# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Everyone contributing to or interacting with KD SOLUTION IT - contributors,
maintainers, and users in any project space - is expected to act with
respect and professionalism.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing real customer data, credentials, or secrets in issues, pull
  requests, or any public space.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any associated chat) and when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to
**conduct@kdsolutionit.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to maintain
confidentiality for the reporter.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code of conduct in
good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by
other members of the project's leadership.
