# 5. Evidence is gathered before the argument starts

**Status:** accepted

## The problem

A card payment can be reversed months later by the payer telling their bank
a short story. The seller is asked for evidence at that point, and whatever
was not recorded at the time cannot be invented afterwards.

## The decision

Everything a card scheme asks for is captured when it happens rather than
when it is needed. The payer ticks a consent statement whose exact words,
time, address, device and language are stored and never edited. Proof of the
delivered work is attached to the invoice and sealed the moment the invoice
is paid. Delivery and opening of the invoice are recorded as events. When a
dispute arrives, the package assembles itself from material that already
existed.

## What this costs

Extra rows on every payment, and a checkout with one more tick box on it.

## What this buys

A dispute answered with a complete file within minutes instead of a week of
searching for emails, and a seller who keeps the money they earned.
